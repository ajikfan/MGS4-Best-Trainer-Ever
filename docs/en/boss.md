# One Shot, One Kill: the bosses

[← Back to the README](../../README.en.md) · *[Version française](../boss.md)*

The general rule of "One Shot, One Kill": in **Lethal**, every hit kills;
in **Non-lethal**, every hit knocks out by draining stamina, without
touching health, whatever the weapon (Mk.2 included). The trainer goes
through the kill planned by the game: phase transitions and fight endings
are still handled by the game.

| Boss | Lethal | Non-lethal |
|---|---|---|
| Laughing Octopus | one hit per phase (lethal weapon: confirmed; non-lethal weapon: prepared, to be confirmed) | stamina drained, one hit per phase, any weapon (confirmed) |
| Laughing Beauty | one hit (confirmed) | stamina drained (confirmed) |
| Raging Raven | one hit (lethal weapon: confirmed; non-lethal weapon: prepared, to be confirmed) | stamina drained, any weapon (confirmed) |
| Raging Beauty | one hit, any weapon (confirmed) | stamina drained (confirmed) |
| Crying Wolf | one hit, any weapon (confirmed) | stamina drained, any weapon (confirmed) |
| Crying Beauty | one hit, any weapon (confirmed) | stamina drained, any weapon (confirmed) |
| Screaming Mantis (Beast and Beauty) | one hit with the doll (confirmed) | stamina drained with the doll (confirmed) |
| Metal Gear RAY | destroyed in one hit (confirmed) | destroyed in one hit (confirmed) |
| Vamp | down in one shot, the syringe is still needed | not covered |
| Liquid Ocelot (final fight) | health and stamina at the phase floor at the first hit | same (fist fight) |

Notes:

- **Screaming Mantis**: only the Mantis doll can reach her, as in the
  game.
- **Liquid Ocelot**: the next hit starts the next phase; the last 7
  scripted hits are not covered.
- **Machines** (Gekko, tanks, helicopters, vehicles, RAY): destroyed in
  both modes; **Dwarf Gekko**: destroyed in Lethal, paralyzed in
  Non-lethal.

The technical details (each boss's damage functions, patches) are in
[How it works](how-it-works.md#one-shot-one-kill--non-lethal).
